# NOT TUBE 1.0.7

Videos now show their real, unmodified frames.

- The player shows every frame of your videos at their own frame rate (30 fps stays 30 fps, no frames dropped)
- Frames are decoded at the player's on-screen size with the video's own aspect ratio, so there's no blur from double scaling, no stretching and no padding baked into the picture
- Colours match the original (HD videos use the correct colour standard)
- Thumbnails are a real frame from your video at its own aspect ratio. Auto thumbnails from older versions are remade once from your original video file; the old images are kept in thumbs\_backup_1.0.6. Thumbnails you picked yourself are never changed
- Try-on haul thumbnails are now real frames from each haul video
- Your video files are never re-encoded or changed

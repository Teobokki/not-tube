# NOT TUBE 1.0.9: meet Not AI

Not AI is NOT TUBE's built-in assistant. Press **Not AI** in the top bar on any page to open it in a side panel next to the player.

It runs on this PC and only uses your library (titles, descriptions, tags, channel names, upload dates, watch history, captions and the video frames). No account, no key, nothing uploaded.

- **Summaries & key moments**: on a video, ask for key moments and get chapters with frame previews. Click a timestamp to jump there. Chapters come from timestamps in the description, otherwise from scene changes found in the frames, otherwise from even segments. Takeaways are the key sentences from the description and captions (.srt/.vtt next to the video, or a subtitle track inside it)
- **Conversational search**: ask in plain words, e.g. "leather jacket hauls under 1 minute" or "newest from Atlas Walks". Length, newest, popular, NOT SHORTS, unwatched and channel names are read from your sentence
- **Title & tag helper**: in Studio > Upload, press "Not AI: title & tags" to clean up a raw filename, get title options and generate tags (new Tags field, also in Edit video)
- **Transparent discovery**: ask for a recommendation. Every pick shows exactly why (your words, your history, channels you NOTSCRIBE to, upload date). No hidden algorithm

Honest note: Not AI doesn't hear speech or recognise what's on screen. It works with words and measured frame changes, and it says so in every answer.

Optional: the gear in the panel lets you add your own AI endpoint to polish wording. It's off by default, and everything works without it.

The 1.0.7 player and thumbnail fixes and the 1.0.8 search bar are unchanged.
